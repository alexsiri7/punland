PUNLAND · STYLE GUIDE · V1

TUNE STORY

Indie dusk. Cartoony, but for teens and up. Flat shapes, two tones, hard shadows.

REFERENCES Return to Monkey Island · Monkey Island 2 cover art · indie travel-poster art (Out of Line, The Islands) Base: Punland Opening Scene, option D
Depth ramp, near to far: #2F2D35 · #4A4A5E · #6E6A82 · #8E879C. Grain overlay about 16%.

From the moodboard

  • Angular characters. Return to Monkey Island: long tapered limbs, pointed snouts and chins, big confident silhouettes. No round, bubbly forms.
  • Poster depth. Out of Line, The Islands: 3–4 flat silhouette layers, each lighter and bluer with distance. No detail in the far layers.
  • Fine grain. A light noise overlay over each scene so the flat colour feels printed, not vector-clean.
  • Warm against cool. Rainy harbour pin: cool blue world, warm pools of shop light. Interiors and windows carry the warmth.

Shading & shadows

  • Light comes from the upper left: a low dusk sun.
  • Two tones per material: base and shade. Shade sits on the right, about 15–20% darker, nudged toward violet.
  • Terminators are hard. No soft blends on objects.
  • Cast shadows: flat #141218 at 45%, hard edge, thrown down and to the right.
  • Light pools: flat polygons of #FFE3A0 or #E8B566 at 7–25%.

Line weight

  • No outer outlines on characters, props or plants. Shapes read by tone.
  • Detail lines use the darkest local colour, never pure black.
  • Only architecture gets a frame stroke: 4–5 px ink #2A1C1A.
  • All weights are at 1280×640 scene scale. Round caps and joins.
