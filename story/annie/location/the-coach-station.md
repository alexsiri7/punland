# The coach station

- **Type:** location
- **Tags:** from-game,act-1,street

Where Paws arrives in Punland: a coach drops him at a bay (think Victoria Coach Station). A walkable street room with the coach bays, a departures board, and a map stand with free maps by the exit. The beetroot cop stands just outside, and the road leads up to Outside The Three Crows. Game hint: "Where every dreamer gets off. Free maps by the exit."
