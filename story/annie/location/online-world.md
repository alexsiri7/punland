# Online world

- **Type:** location
- **Tags:** from-game

A visual version of running a band's Instagram: chat to people, send announcements, take pictures, talk to influencers who can repost you. Full of visual puns.

## Notes

In Act 1 this is the band's page, empty ("No name, no posts, no followers. Yet."). The game locks it at the start: "The band needs a name before it can go online." (decided in the game; open in Annie).
