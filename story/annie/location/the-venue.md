# The venue

- **Type:** location

Bar/venue where the protagonist plays; the bartender works here; guarded by the bouncer. A very famous place where great stars played. Has a Wall of Fame with photos of big bands that played there, as visual puns: the Rolling Stones (a pile of rocks), the Beetles (beetles with 60s haircuts), Tuna and Pike Turner, Katty Purry. The Three Crows hang out here. Name TBD (placeholder: The Root Note).
