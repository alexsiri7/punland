# The Three Crows

- **Type:** location
- **Tags:** from-game,act-1,street

The pub and venue where the protagonist plays: a big, leaning Victorian corner pub. The bartender works here; guarded by the bouncer. A very famous place where great stars played. Has a Wall of Fame with photos of big bands that played there, as visual puns: the Rolling Stones (a pile of rocks), the Beetles (beetles with 60s haircuts), Tuna and Pike Turner, Katty Purry. The Three Crows sit behind the bar, and there is a stage where the rival performs. Downstairs: The Holding Pen.

## Notes

Renamed from "The venue" on 2026-11-15. The name The Three Crows was decided in the game and every Act 1 draft uses it (it replaces the placeholder The Root Note). "Big leaning Victorian corner pub" is the game's look (style guide).
