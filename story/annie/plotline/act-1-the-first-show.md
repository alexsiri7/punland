# Act 1 — The first show

- **Type:** plotline

Goal: get to the first show.

Opening: he lands and tells a beetroot cop on the street "I'm X, and I want to be a music star". The cop doesn't know much about that, but says all the music stars go to "the three crows", and points him there.

At the pub, the Three Crows must all agree to let him perform. Each sets a condition:
1. A working guitar: fix the broken string. At the music shop, the dog (the rival) is buying the last string. For a different reason, he's told to go and see the Mayor (a mare), who makes a string out of her mane.
2. A good song of his own:
- On the High Street he sees the Song Master's info. The Song Master lives in a flat on the High Street; you press the intercom and get invited in.
- The Song Master gives everyone the same lesson and the same basic rhyming song (cutscene-style). The cat plans to play it to the second crow.
- He goes to the pub to ask to play it, but the dog is already on stage playing the exact same song.
- He runs out saying he doesn't feel well, thinking he needs to write a new song. The Song Master no longer answers the doorbell.
- That's when he needs to start dreaming for songs.
3. Someone who lives here: the third crow says "I won't vouch for you". The bartender (love interest) says she can convince the third crow, if he's going to stay. She gets him a job in the pub's kitchen. (Earlier idea: he needs a place to stay and a job; is a place to stay still needed?)

The rival (the dog) is in Act 1 and always one step ahead.

Earlier idea: he uses the pub job to request a slot at the very end of the night.
