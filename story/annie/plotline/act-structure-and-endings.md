# Act structure and endings

- **Type:** plotline

Four acts, Monkey Island style. Acts 1–3 are the same story for everyone, though attributes like popularity can influence them. Act 4 depends on earlier decisions, so there are different Act 4 stories leading to different endings, e.g. solo social media influencer, restaurant player, international rock band. Options open up when you collect enough of something: followers, good musicians, practice, good instruments.
