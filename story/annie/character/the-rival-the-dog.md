# The rival (the dog)

- **Type:** character
- **Role:** antagonist

The rival is a dog (male singer). Appears in Act 1 and is always one step ahead of the cat: buys the last guitar string at the music shop, and is on stage at the pub playing the exact same song the Song Master taught the cat. By the end of Act 2 the cat is beating him, so he frames the cat for something (Act 3). Act 4 ends in a song-off with him.
