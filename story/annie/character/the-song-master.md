# The Song Master

- **Type:** character
- **Role:** supporting

A rat. Wise old master type: Alex is thinking of Splinter, but also very Yoda-like (inspiration only; the character itself is original). Songwriting trainer, in the spirit of the sword-fighting trainer in Monkey Island. His info is seen on the High Street (earlier idea: a sign in the music shop). Lives in a flat on the High Street; you press the intercom and get invited in. Name TBD.

The lesson is like a cutscene. He always gives the same lesson and the same basic, rhyming song to everyone. Alex's example of the style:
"I came to the city / to find my dream / my guitar is broken / my clothes ain't clean"
The dog took the same lesson and used the song directly, which was exactly the cat's plan too. After that, the Song Master stops answering the doorbell (why: still open).
