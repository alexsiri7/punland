# The Three Crows

- **Type:** character
- **Role:** supporting

Three crows behind the bar of the pub who decide who gets to perform. The cat must convince all three. One wants a working guitar, one wants a good song of his own, one wants someone who lives in Punland. The third says "I won't vouch for you"; the bartender can convince him if the cat is staying. One of them is Sheryl Caw.
